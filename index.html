HEMU CREATIONS / LEGAL

Terms & policies.

01 / Scope

Every project starts with an agreed brief, deliverables, timeline and price. Requests outside the agreed scope may require a revised estimate.

02 / Revisions

Revision rounds are agreed before work begins. Feedback should be shared as one clear, consolidated note per round.

03 / Payment

Deposits or milestone payments, when applicable, are confirmed in writing before the project starts.

04 / Delivery

Final working files are delivered after the agreed work and payment are complete. Third-party assets stay subject to their own licences.

05 / Timeline

Schedules depend on receiving the brief, assets and feedback on time. Changes or cancellation are discussed for each project.

06 / Privacy

Reference files and project details are used only to discuss and deliver the requested work. Share content you have permission to provide.

These notes are a general starting point. The written proposal/project agreement for your specific project takes priority.
